# Problem Tension Canvas

A strong project starts with a visible tension.

## Tension Pattern

Every [action] exposes [sensitive thing].
That creates [risk/problem].
The project fixes this by proving [rule] without revealing [data].

## Examples

Every payment is visible. That’s a problem.
Every regulated asset requires KYC. That’s a problem.
Subscriptions charge for unused access. That’s a problem.
Bug bounty evidence can expose PoCs too early. That’s a problem.
Privacy payments need compliance. That’s a problem.

Every invoice settlement can expose sensitive business data.
BatchGuard fixes this by proving payment-policy compliance without revealing invoice amounts or vendor risk scores.

## Checklist

[ ] The problem is concrete
[ ] The sensitive thing is named
[ ] The consequence is clear
[ ] The proof rule is clear
[ ] The hidden data is clear
[ ] The sentence can fit in a tweet/post
