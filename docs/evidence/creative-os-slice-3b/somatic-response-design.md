## Fixture A: Luxurious (Premium Perfume Landing Page)
* **Descriptor**: luxurious
* **Subject**: premium perfume landing page
* **Context**: landing page

### 1. Physiological Profile
* **Observable Body Reaction**: gradual deepening of breath, micro-pause at boundaries, shoulder lowering
* **Observable Viewer Behavior**: lingering gaze, slower swipe speed, tactile-like hovering over elements
* **Eye Path**: undulating, serpentine path following subtle visual anchors; avoids hard grid lines
* **Viewing Speed**: deliberate, slow (unhurried navigation)
* **Focal Duration**: extended (1.2s to 2.5s per hero cluster)
* **Posture / Approach Behavior**: slight backward lean (relaxation/absorption)
* **Micro-reaction**: softening of facial muscles, micro-smile of satisfaction
* **Tension Level**: low

### 2. Composition & Sensory Consequences
* **Composition**: highly asymmetric, fluid visual flow with overlapping scent-story imagery
* **Density**: ultra-low density; single scent bottle and raw ingredient shot per screen height
* **Whitespace**: vast breathing margins (55%+ screen area) acting as physical vacuum of elegance
* **Scale**: extreme contrast; very large scale headlines paired with tiny metadata tags
* **Typography**: high-contrast classic editorial serif with extreme vertical character ratio
* **Color Temperature**: soft alabaster, warm linen, gold accents, glass-like reflection details
* **Motion Tempo**: slow, continuous ease-in-out transforms; 1.2s-2.0s duration transitions
* **Sound**: low-frequency atmospheric pads, delicate organic instrumentation
* **Accessibility Safeguard**: preserve 4.5:1 contrast on active interactive boundaries despite low density

### 3. Verification & Validation
* **Five-Second Validation Test**: Show for 5 seconds: does the user feel the design evokes an olfactory and tactile sensory premium?
* **Failure Signals**:
  * viewer swipes rapidly within 2 seconds
  * eye path jumps sporadically
  * subject describes it as "sterile"
* **Quality Gate Results**:
  * Physical Vocabulary Present: PASS (breath, shoulder, muscle, gaze)
  * Art Direction Guidance Concrete: PASS (composition, color, and typography are defined and concrete)
  * Risk Areas Identified: PASS (3 failure signals mapped)
  * No Coercive Patterns Safeguard: PASS (safeguards contrast)

## Fixture B: Bold (B2B SaaS Analytics Dashboard)
* **Descriptor**: bold
* **Subject**: B2B SaaS analytics dashboard
* **Context**: dashboard

### 1. Physiological Profile
* **Observable Body Reaction**: micro-startle response, sharp inhale, pupil dilation
* **Observable Viewer Behavior**: direct visual confrontation, rapid scan of center weight, aggressive scrolling
* **Eye Path**: highly linear, central targeting; moves directly from primary weight to secondary details
* **Viewing Speed**: high speed (rapid consumption)
* **Focal Duration**: short, intense (0.4s to 0.8s per hero cluster)
* **Posture / Approach Behavior**: forward lean (engagement/confrontation)
* **Micro-reaction**: jaw tightening, brief eyebrow raise
* **Tension Level**: high

### 2. Composition & Sensory Consequences
* **Composition**: brutal block layout, heavy margins, hard borders
* **Density**: medium-high; high-contrast elements stacked directly
* **Whitespace**: functional, compressed margins; negative space serves as sharp borders
* **Scale**: maximal scale; oversized display titles (96px+) occupying major screen blocks
* **Typography**: ultra-bold geometric sans-serifs, compressed widths
* **Color Temperature**: high-contrast saturation; pure blacks, acid yellow, vermilion, cool grey base
* **Motion Tempo**: instantaneous transitions (0ms-150ms), hard-cut reveals, spring-loaded step animations
* **Sound**: percussive, sharp transients, fast-decay envelopes
* **Accessibility Safeguard**: prevent flashing indicators above 3Hz; enforce legible subtitle tracks

### 3. Verification & Validation
* **Five-Second Validation Test**: Show for 5 seconds: does the viewer remember the central core claim exactly? (Boldness must focus attention, not scatter it.)
* **Failure Signals**:
  * viewer squints or looks away
  * viewer fails to recall the primary action
  * subject describes it as "noisy"

## Fixture C: Eye-Catching (Premium Home Renovation Service)
* **Descriptor**: eye-catching
* **Subject**: premium home renovation service
* **Context**: renovation service

### 1. Physiological Profile
* **Observable Body Reaction**: saccadic capture, rapid eye fixation shift, momentary breathing suspension
* **Observable Viewer Behavior**: immediate stop on visual anomaly, pointer tracking toward the anomaly
* **Eye Path**: radial outward; initial capture point followed by rapid surrounding context scan
* **Viewing Speed**: dynamic (stop-and-scan patterns)
* **Focal Duration**: medium (0.8s to 1.5s on the anomaly)
* **Posture / Approach Behavior**: head tilt, momentary freeze in movement
* **Micro-reaction**: widening of eyes, head alignment adjust
* **Tension Level**: medium

### 2. Composition & Sensory Consequences
* **Composition**: off-grid focal point, overlapping elements, visual anomaly breaking the pattern
* **Density**: medium; isolated visual interest center in a clean grid
* **Whitespace**: high surrounding whitespace to isolate the visual capture element
* **Scale**: moderate baseline scale, high relative scale for the capture trigger
* **Typography**: stylized custom glyphs, neon-styled headers
* **Color Temperature**: vibrant accent pops (electric blue, lime green) against dark slate or deep grey backdrop
* **Motion Tempo**: playful micro-animations, ripple effects, scroll-linked rotation
* **Sound**: rising tone sweeps, subtle high-frequency bells
* **Accessibility Safeguard**: ensure all motion can be disabled via standard reduced-motion preferences

### 3. Verification & Validation
* **Five-Second Validation Test**: Show for 5 seconds: does the visual anomaly capture the first 2 seconds of gaze?
* **Failure Signals**:
  * viewer misses the capture element entirely
  * viewer describes it as "gimmicky"
  * loss of reading flow

* **Subject**: mindfulness audio narrative device
* **Context**: audio companion app

### 1. Physiological Profile
* **Observable Body Reaction**: respiratory deceleration, muscle relaxation, neutral brow
* **Observable Viewer Behavior**: passive reading, gentle scrolling, minimal mouse movement
* **Eye Path**: smooth horizontal sweeps, scanning top-to-bottom sequentially
* **Viewing Speed**: slow (meditative reading)
* **Focal Duration**: long (1.5s to 3.0s per text block)
* **Posture / Approach Behavior**: relaxed lean back (reflective posture)
* **Micro-reaction**: deep exhalation, quiet focus
* **Tension Level**: low

### 2. Composition & Sensory Consequences
* **Composition**: balanced symmetry, horizontal bands, aligned blocks
* **Density**: low density; limited options per view, clear priority
* **Whitespace**: very high; open space acting as silence
* **Scale**: consistent, gentle scale steps (no loud visual hierarchy jumps)
* **Typography**: highly legible classic serif (e.g. Georgia, Lora) or clean neutral sans-serif
* **Color Temperature**: cool, low-saturation earth tones; slate, moss, sage, warm cream backing
* **Motion Tempo**: slow fade-in/out transitions, 800ms ease duration (no sudden translates)
* **Sound**: continuous ambient textures, silence-dominated soundscapes
* **Accessibility Safeguard**: ensure high contrast for body copy; absolute avoidance of moving elements behind text

### 3. Verification & Validation
* **Five-Second Validation Test**: Show for 5 seconds: does the viewer feel an absence of pressure to act immediately?
* **Failure Signals**:
  * viewer feels bored or drops off
  * viewer describes it as "clinical" or "sad"
