# Cognitive Metaphor Illustrator Fixtures Report

Translates abstract concepts into mechanical objects and forces, actively auditing and rejecting high-risk clichés (e.g. plants for growth, lightbulbs for innovation).

---

## Fixture A: Technical Debt
* **Concept**: technical debt
* **Audience**: product managers
* **Cliché Risk Audit**:
  * Cliché plant/gardening: **HIGH RISK** (rejected).
  * Decaying architectural foundations: **LOW RISK** (selected).
* **Scene Proposition**: A heavy granite block supported by a row of pillars where three pillars are replaced by temporary wooden sticks bowing under the weight.
* **Cognitive Action**: Show load shifting from designed structures to inadequate temporary workarounds.

---

## Fixture B: Trust Erosion
* **Concept**: trust erosion
* **Audience**: executive sponsors
* **Cliché Risk Audit**:
  * Cliché shield/lock: **HIGH RISK** (rejected).
  * Sandstone arch under droplets: **LOW RISK** (selected).
* **Scene Proposition**: A solid stone archway showing a deep groove worn into the capstone by a slow, single-source drip of water.
* **Cognitive Action**: Expose slow, structural wear from single-source continuous friction.

---

## Fixture C: Operational Bottleneck
* **Concept**: operational bottleneck
* **Audience**: operations leaders
* **Cliché Risk Audit**:
  * Cliché funnel/traffic jam: **HIGH RISK** (rejected).
  * Gravity-fed sphere runway: **LOW RISK** (selected).
* **Scene Proposition**: A sloping track where metal balls roll down; the track narrows to let only one ball pass, while three other balls accumulate behind it.
