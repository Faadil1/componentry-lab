# Cognitive Metaphor Illustrator Fixtures Report

Translates abstract concepts into mechanical objects and forces, actively auditing and rejecting high-risk clichés (e.g. plants for growth, lightbulbs for innovation) and literalized concepts.

---

## Fixture A: Technical Debt
* **Concept**: technical debt
* **Audience**: product managers
* **Cliché & Literalization Risk Audit**:
  * Cliché plant/gardening: **HIGH RISK** (rejected, transformation: LOW_TRANSFORMATION).
  * Decaying architectural foundations: **LOW RISK** (selected, transformation: STRUCTURAL_ANALOGY, literalization risk: 1/10).
  * Over-packed cargo boat: **LOW RISK** (alternative, transformation: STRUCTURAL_ANALOGY).
* **Scene Proposition**: A heavy granite block supported by a row of pillars where three pillars are replaced by temporary wooden sticks bowing under the weight.
* **Cognitive Action**: Show load shifting from designed structures to inadequate temporary workarounds.
