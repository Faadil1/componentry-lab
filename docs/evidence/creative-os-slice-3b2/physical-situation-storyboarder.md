# Physical Situation Storyboarder Fixtures Report

The **Physical Situation Storyboarder** converts abstract narrative concepts into physical situations through:
$$\text{Character/Subject} + \text{Real Object/Environment} + \text{Physical Action} + \text{Spatial Relationship} + \text{Observable Consequence}$$

---

## Fixture A: existential anxiety / incompleteness
* **Subject**: Mara pottery sequence
* **Context**: Short film
* **Physical Metaphor**: A physical struggle with a tangible, stubborn object that resists simple completion.
* **Nodes**:
  1. `beat_01_setup`: Mara sits 20cm from an unfinished clay vessel on the wheel. She hovers her hands without touching.
  2. `beat_02_tension`: Mara reaches for a smoothing tool, holds it for 3 seconds, then lays it down untouched.
  3. `beat_03_transformation`: Mara turns away and walks out of frame, leaving the unfinished vessel in the center.
* **Unspoken Subtext**: "Do not speak or mention existential anxiety or acceptance."
